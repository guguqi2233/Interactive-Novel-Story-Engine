# Action Mod Contract

Current Action Mod contract version: `1.8`.

Declarative actions are data-only definitions with id, label, aliases,
category, contract version, targets, affordances, time cost, preconditions,
checks, outcomes, StateDelta templates, event type, visibility policy, and
narrator hints.

Action Mods do not execute code, do not call LLMs, and do not bypass StateDelta
or EventLog.
